Matthew's Argentina made the Final
Argentina marched through the tournament with style — 9 points in Group J, wins over Egypt and Switzerland in the knockouts — only to fall at the last hurdle to Spain. A brilliant tournament for Matthew's team, worth €25 and a story Argentina fans will dine out on for a decade.
